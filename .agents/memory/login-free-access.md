---
name: Login-free access
description: The product intentionally has no authentication while a future login system is deferred.
---

Keep every product capability available without authentication, including dialogue, voice transcription, visitor analytics, diagnostics, uploads, timeline, and management/database mutations.

**Why:** The owner explicitly removed the previous Google login as a temporary product decision and required zero loss or restriction of functionality. A different login system may be created later.

**How to apply:** Do not restore the old Google OAuth flow or add hidden owner/admin gates. If authentication is requested later, treat it as a new system and confirm which capabilities, if any, should become restricted.
